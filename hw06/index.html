This assignment gave me my first hands-on experience with AI text-to-video generation. I used ComfyUI together with open-source models from Civitai to create video content through a node-based workflow. The process was very interesting because I had to connect different nodes, adjust settings, and experiment with prompts to achieve better results. Through this project, I learned that prompt design and model selection have a significant impact on the final video quality. I also encountered some challenges, such as maintaining consistency between frames, but solving these issues helped me better understand how generative AI works. Overall, this assignment was both educational and enjoyable. It improved my technical skills, creativity, and understanding of modern AI video generation tools.
